Aviator is a browser flight game built with three.js r160 and vanilla JavaScript.

Everything you see and hear is generated at runtime: terrain, clouds, aircraft, effects, engine sound and music. There are no image or audio files.

Flight model: rigid-body 6-DOF with lift, drag (parasitic + induced), side force, propeller thrust, stall, spin, ground contact and damage.

The crash round uses a provably-fair, seeded SHA-256 scheme. All balances are play money and have no value.

Design, code and procedural art: generated for this project. Thanks for flying!

Before each round you see hash = SHA-256(seed). After the crash the seed is revealed. The crash point is floor(100·(1−edge)/(1−r))/100 where r is the first 52 bits of SHA-256(seed:clientSeed) ÷ 2⁵². Seeds come from a seeded chain: seed = SHA-256(masterSeed:nonce).
